# Domain Glossary (`GLOSSARY.md`)

> Canonical definitions of domain terms, entities, and bounded-context concepts for **SaaS Stock Deposit Control (Audit Global)**.  
> Both Antigravity (specs) and Claude Code (implementation) must use these exact terms consistently in specs, code symbols, APIs, and tests.

| Term | Definition | Aliases to Avoid | Bounded Context / Module |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Enterprise (`tenant_id`)** | Top-level organization/tenant owning branches, users, and analytical records (`db_users.enterprises`, `db_analytics.record_tenants`). | `Company`, `Organization`, `Client` | Identity & Multi-Tenancy (`app/enterprises`, `db_users`) |
| **Branch (`id_branch` / `id_sucursal`)** | Physical pharmacy store or operational location belonging to an Enterprise (`db_users.branches`). | `Store`, `Location`, `Shop` | Identity & Multi-Tenancy (`app/branches`, `db_users`) |
| **Deposit (`id_deposito`)** | Logical or physical warehouse storage area inside a Branch where stock positions and FEFO lots are held (`db_articles.depositos`). | `Warehouse`, `Bin`, `Shelf` | Catalog & Stock (`app/articles`, `app/movements`, `db_articles`) |
| **Article (`id_articulo`)** | Master catalog SKU (`db_articles.articulos`) classified by Family (`id_familia`), Group (`id_grupo`), Brand (`id_marca`), EAN barcodes (`codigos_barra_articulo`), and unit purchase cost without VAT (`pcio_com_siva`). | `Product`, `Item`, `SKU` | Catalog (`app/articles`, `db_articles`) |
| **Purchase Cost Without VAT (`pcio_com_siva`)** | Confidential unit purchase price excluding VAT (`Decimal`), exposed strictly to the `administrador` role and structurally omitted for `supervisor` and `auditor`. | `unit_price`, `cost`, `price` | Catalog & Financial Privacy (`app/articles`, `app/reports`) |
| **Theoretical Stock (`stock_real`)** | System-recorded baseline quantity of an Article in a Deposit (`db_articles.stock_por_deposito`) against which physical counts are compared. | `system_stock`, `expected_stock` | Stock & Movements (`app/movements`, `db_articles`) |
| **Physical Count (`stock_fisico_contado`)** | Quantity of an Article physically counted by an operator on the warehouse floor during an audit. | `actual_stock`, `counted_stock` | Auditing (`app/movements`, `frontend-web`, `frontend-mobile`) |
| **Stock Discrepancy (`delta_stock`)** | Signed difference between physical count and theoretical stock ($\text{stock\_fisico\_contado} - \text{stock\_real}$). | `variance`, `diff` | Auditing (`DeltaCalculator`, `app/movements`) |
| **Action Type (`tipo_accion`)** | Voucher classification derived from `delta_stock`: **`Alta`** ($\Delta > 0$, surplus / capital addition), **`Baja`** ($\Delta < 0$, shrinkage / financial loss), or **`Sin Acción`** ($\Delta = 0$, exact match). | `movement_type`, `adjustment_type` | Auditing (`app/movements`, `shared/design-tokens`) |
| **Valorized Amount (`monto_valorizado`)** | Monetary value (`Decimal`) of an audit discrepancy computed from `pcio_com_siva`, VAT rate, and `cantidad_afectada`. Masked for non-admin roles. | `total_cost`, `variance_amount` | Auditing & Analytics (`app/movements`, `app/reports`, `db_analytics`) |
| **FEFO (`First Expired, First Out`)** | Lot management rule prioritizing batches (`lotes_por_deposito`) by earliest expiration date (`fec_vto`), classified into `VENCIDO` (expired), `PRÓXIMO` ($\le 90$ days), or `NORMAL` ($> 90$ days). | `FIFO`, `LIFO` | Catalog & Auditing (`FefoEvaluator`, `db_articles`) |
| **Work Session (`WorkSession`)** | Active branch-scoped operational audit window (`status = "OPEN"`) in `db_analytics.work_sessions` under which `AuditRecord` entries are grouped. | `AuditRun`, `Shift`, `BatchSession` | Work Sessions (`app/work_sessions`, `db_analytics`) |
| **Shrinkage Report (`Mermas`)** | Executive financial report (`/api/v1/reports/shrinkage`) aggregating `Baja` discrepancies with fiscal VAT rules (`0%` for `Medicamentos`, `21%` default for other families). | `LossReport`, `WasteReport` | Reports (`ShrinkageReportService`, `app/reports`) |
| **Goods Received Report (`Ingresos / Altas`)** | Executive financial report (`/api/v1/reports/goods-received`) aggregating `Alta` additions and calculating total injected capital (`capital_inyectado`). | `AdditionsReport`, `InflowReport` | Reports (`GoodsReceivedReportService`, `app/reports`) |
| **Daily Inventory Balance (`Balance Diario`)** | Consolidated daily ledger (`/api/v1/reports/inventory-balance`) computing Net Patrimonial Variation ($\Delta \text{Patrimonial} = \text{Total Altas} - \text{Total Mermas}$). | `DailySummary`, `LedgerReport` | Reports (`DailyInventoryBalanceReportService`, `app/reports`) |
| **Hybrid On-Premise Topology** | Deployment architecture where `db_articles` connects read-only (`SELECT` only, `RUN_MIGRATIONS_ARTICLES=false`) to the pharmacy's existing MariaDB server while `db_users` and `db_analytics` run in the local bundled MariaDB container (`with-db`). | `SplitDB`, `ExternalCatalog` | DevOps & Infrastructure (`docs/deploy/local-onpremise-runbook.md`) |
